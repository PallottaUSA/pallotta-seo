Who We Are

Paver & Concrete Contractor Serving Pasco County, FL

At Pallotta Pavers & Concrete LLC, we are proud to be recognized as a top provider of high-quality concrete, natural stone, and hardscaping services throughout Pasco County and northern Hillsborough County. From Wesley Chapel and Land O’ Lakes to Zephyrhills, Dade City, Spring Hill and Brooksville, we've built a strong reputation for expert craftsmanship, reliable service, and exceptional value across every project. We are licensed in Pasco County under C.C # LC-009985, insured and bonded.

Built for Central Florida conditions

Our mission is simple: to deliver outstanding results at competitive rates, while ensuring long-term durability and strength in every build. Central Florida's ever-changing weather demands materials and techniques that last. That's why our skilled team uses proven construction practices and high-grade materials to create surfaces that are not only beautiful, but also built to withstand the elements year-round.

Clear communication from quote to walkthrough
