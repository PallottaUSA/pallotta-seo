Paver & Concrete Services
Patios, Driveways & Pool Decks

Pallotta Pavers & Concrete installs paver patios, driveways, pool decks, walkways, steps and retaining walls, and pours concrete driveways, slabs, RV pads and sidewalks. We serve Pasco County and northern Hillsborough County, with more than 30 years in the trade.

Every job starts with a free site visit. We check your grade, drainage and soil before quoting, so the number you get is the number you pay.

Paver Installation

Interlocking pavers, natural stone and travertine, set on a compacted and drained base. Pavers move with the ground instead of fighting it, so they tend to outlast a poured slab in Florida soil.

Patios

A paver patio holds its shape through Florida heat and heavy summer rain. If a paver ever gets stained or damaged, we lift that one and replace it. No patch lines, no tearing out the whole patio.

Driveways

Paver driveways sit on a compacted base built for vehicle loads, with a contrasting border course around the edge. They look finished from the street and will not spider-crack the way a poured slab can.

Hardscapes

We plan patio, walkway, walls and beds together instead of one piece at a time. That means grading and drainage are sorted once, and the materials meet cleanly where they change.

Steps

Paver and stone steps built to even riser heights on a compacted, drained base. Even risers are what keep steps feeling safe underfoot years later.

Retaining Walls

Retaining walls hold back grade and turn a slope into level, usable yard. Every wall gets proper base prep, backfill and drainage behind the face so water does not push it out over time.

Pool Decks

Pool decks need a surface that stays cooler underfoot, drains fast and shrugs off chlorine. Pavers and travertine do all three, and they lift out for pool or plumbing access without breaking concrete.

Fire Pits

A built-in fire pit gets a patio used after dark and through the cooler months. We build them in matching or contrasting block so it looks like it was always part of the plan.

Also offered

Commercial Pavers

Entries, courtyards and drive aprons built for constant traffic. A section can be lifted and repaired without closing the whole area to customers.

Poured Concrete

Forming, pouring and finishing, from a garage floor to a parking area. We use strong mixes and put control joints where they belong, so any future cracking follows the lines we planned.

RV Pads

RV and boat pads poured at the thickness and reinforcement your load actually needs, on a base compacted so it does not settle. We slope them to push water off the pad and away from the house.

Driveways

Poured driveways with a compacted base, the right thickness for vehicle loads and saw-cut joints on a planned grid. Finishes run from plain broom to decorative.

Sidewalks

Walkways, sidewalks and entry landings poured to even width and slope, with enough cross-fall to shed water instead of ponding it against the house.

Seamless Texture Concrete

Seamless texture and stamped finishes give a poured slab the look of stone or tile with no joint lines to trap dirt. We apply the texture while the concrete is still workable, then seal it.

Also offered

Garage Floors

Garage floors placed and finished flat, with control joints set so any future shrinkage cracks follow those lines instead of running across the middle.

Pole Barn / Metal Building Slabs

Slabs for pole barns and metal buildings, formed to the building footprint with anchor bolts, thickened edges and reinforcement matched to the structure going on top.

Parking Lots

Parking areas poured for constant vehicle loading, with jointing, drainage fall and reinforcement worked out before the first truck arrives.
